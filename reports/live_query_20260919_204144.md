**Question:** What accuracy did the VGG16 and CNN models achieve for lung cancer CT scan classification, and which performed better?

## Answer

In the primary comparative study evaluated, the **VGG16** architecture achieved the highest classification performance with a **test accuracy of 98.18%** for three-class lung cancer CT scan classification (Normal, Benign, and Malignant) [AI-Powered Lung Cancer Detection- Assessing VGG16 and CNN Architectures for CT Scan Image Classification.pdf, p.1, p.25].

The other CNN architectures evaluated in the benchmark achieved the following test accuracies:
- **MobileNetV2**: **93.64%** test accuracy (with a training accuracy of 97.15%) [AI-Powered Lung Cancer Detection- Assessing VGG16 and CNN Architectures for CT Scan Image Classification.pdf, p.13, p.25]
- **ResNet50**: **93.33%** test accuracy [AI-Powered Lung Cancer Detection- Assessing VGG16 and CNN Architectures for CT Scan Image Classification.pdf, p.25]
- **InceptionV3**: **88.79%** test accuracy [AI-Powered Lung Cancer Detection- Assessing VGG16 and CNN Architectures for CT Scan Image Classification.pdf, p.25]

Additionally, related prior studies cited in the paper reported the following accuracies for lung cancer CT scan classification:
- **Aharonu and Kumar** (CNN framework): **94.11%** accuracy [AI-Powered Lung Cancer Detection- Assessing VGG16 and CNN Architectures for CT Scan Image Classification.pdf, p.3]
- **Zargar et al.** (VGG16 model): **91%** accuracy [AI-Powered Lung Cancer Detection- Assessing VGG16 and CNN Architectures for CT Scan Image Classification.pdf, p.3]
- **Bherje et al.** (CNN-based framework): **72.41%** average accuracy [AI-Powered Lung Cancer Detection- Assessing VGG16 and CNN Architectures for CT Scan Image Classification.pdf, p.3]

**VGG16 performed better** than traditional and other CNN architectures for lung cancer CT scan classification [AI-Powered Lung Cancer Detection- Assessing VGG16 and CNN Architectures for CT Scan Image Classification.pdf, p.1, p.3].

Key details from the studies include:
- **Comparison to other CNN architectures:** When benchmarked against other CNN architectures (ResNet50, MobileNetV2, and InceptionV3) on a three-class CT classification task (Normal, Benign, Malignant), VGG16 achieved the highest overall test accuracy at **98.18%** (compared to 93.64% for MobileNetV2, 93.33% for ResNet50, and 88.79% for InceptionV3) and showed superior detection for malignant cases [AI-Powered Lung Cancer Detection- Assessing VGG16 and CNN Architectures for CT Scan Image Classification.pdf, p.13, p.25].
- **Comparison to traditional/basic CNNs:** Prior research by Xu demonstrated that while basic/traditional CNN models achieved acceptable accuracy, VGG16 outperformed them in learning efficiency, overall accuracy, and feature extraction capabilities due to its deeper network structure [AI-Powered Lung Cancer Detection- Assessing VGG16 and CNN Architectures for CT Scan Image Classification.pdf, p.3, p.13].
